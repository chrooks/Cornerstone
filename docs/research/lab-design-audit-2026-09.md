## Weakest Signifiers

1. **Letter grades on a hidden scale.** Six F tiles on a 3.51★ Rotation. A red F sits next to an amber 5.6. The letter, tile tint, number color and narrative use four different scales (ranks 5, 15).
2. **"Value" with no meaning, and a false FAQ.** "Value" reads as quality, but it is a cap cost. The FAQ still says $54M (rank 2).
3. **The Final Scouting Note speaks as the engine.** It is open by default, placed above Pressure Points and not labeled as AI. It contradicts the grades: "elite rim-pressure system" next to Rim Pressure F 5.4 (ranks 4, 14).
4. **Mouse words and hover-only reads on touch.** "Left-click to add · Right-click…" and "Hover a factor…" show on a phone. The salary ghost, eval preview and native-title explainers never open there (ranks 6, 24).
5. **Grey 0.00 stars** still frame the start as failure (rank 8).
6. **Illegal Builds look legal.** An over-cap Rotation scores 3.57, higher than the legal 3.51, and nothing on Final Eval says "cap" (rank 9).
7. **Bad links go silent.** Skeletons that never end, a blank Build, a raw backend string, a silent price fallback, dropped players (rank 10).

## Progressive Disclosure Risks

- Final Eval runs 3,829 px on desktop and about 5,900 px on phone. A ~400-word note opens by default. Pressure Points, the shortest read, comes last (rank 4).
- July's "everything at once" is fixed on Final Eval by the cause-reveal. The Build still lands a change all at once (refuted `build-prior-fix4`). That is acceptable for a live loop.
- Explainers live in native `title` tooltips, so touch users get no disclosure at all (rank 24).
- The expert ceiling moved up one level. Subscores explain themselves, but the Score Factor weights that make 3.51 do not (rank 37). Nine tiles still have no ledger (rank 33).
- The Build hands out verdicts ("Spacing 4.7 F", "Lineup-level passing is thin") with no path until Final Eval (rank 29).
- Legends leads with the full `/players` query builder for 36 Legends (rank 47). The Player Shape, the best comparison tool, is one click deep (rank 39).

## Design Boundary Violations

- **Grades still overclaim** (persisting from July). The ledger now defends each number, but nothing defends the letter. "F below 6.0 of 10" makes most real Builds look like failures (ranks 5, 15).
- **The AI note claims the engine's authority** and invents history: "these five have played together enough that their defensive reads … feel natural" ([phone-07 full](lab-audit-screens-2026-09/phone-07-eval-full.png)). It needs an AI label and grade-bound adjectives (ranks 4, 14).
- **Illegal Builds are scored as normal,** higher than the legal Build, with no RuleSet context on the page (rank 9).
- **Unrated players look like real options** at $0 (rank 3).
- **"League percentiles" for a 1991 Legend** does not say which league (rank 57).
